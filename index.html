📜 OFFICIAL SERVER & LEADERBOARD RULES

1. Real-Time In-Game Tracking: All player stats, kills, deaths, and ELO ratings are registered live from our MCPE 0.15.10 PocketMine server.

2. Fair Play & Modded Clients: Hacks, reach modifications, auto-clickers, macro tools, or illegal client patches are strictly prohibited.

3. Kill Farming & Exploits: Intentionally killing alternate accounts (alts), AFK players, or trading kills to boost ELO points is banned.

4. Screen Recording Verification: Official competitive duels require full, unedited screen recordings to be submitted upon staff request.

📈 HOW RANKINGS & ELO WORK

1. Real-Time Server Sync: Killing a player inside the server calculates new ELO points and pushes the update directly to `data.json`.

2. Tier Bands (C to S+): Players are grouped automatically into tier bands based on rating.

3. Automatic ELO Sorting: Inside each band, players are ordered from highest to lowest ELO rating.

4. Auto-Refresh: Website polls for updates every 5 minutes to keep standings fresh.
