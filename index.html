📜 OFFICIAL SERVER & LEADERBOARD RULES

1. In-Game Automatic Tracking: All player stats, kills, deaths, and win streaks are calculated live from our MCPE 0.15.10 PocketMine server. Whenever a player dies in combat, the server plugin verifies the kill and syncs the score instantly to this site.

2. Fair Play & Modded Clients: Hacks, reach modifications, auto-clickers, macro tools, or illegitimate client patches are strictly banned. Any player caught abusing glitches will face a permanent global ELO wipe and an IP ban from the server.

3. Kill Farming & Exploits: Intentionally killing alternate accounts (alts), AFK players, or trading kills to boost ELO points is prohibited. Suspicious match records are audited by server staff.

4. Screen Recording Verification: High-tier official duels (FT7) require full unedited screen recordings to be submitted to our official Discord server upon staff request.

📈 HOW RANKINGS & ELO WORK

1. Real-Time Server Sync: As soon as you land a final hit on an enemy player inside the server, the server plugin pushes your updated kill count and win streak directly to GitHub Pages.

2. Dynamic Tier Progression: Tiers are sorted automatically based on your overall ELO rating and total kills. Earning enough points upgrades your rank badge automatically from C Tier all the way up to S+ Tier.

3. Kill Streaks & Multipliers: Maintaining a continuous win streak without dying grants extra ELO points per kill, making high-tier ladder pushes faster.

4. Instant Leaderboard Re-sorting: The site automatically re-calculates player ranks every time the data updates, displaying real-time movements across all tiers.
