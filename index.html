📜 How Ranking Up Works
To move up the leaderboards and claim a higher rank position, you must challenge and FT7 (First To 7 Wins) players placed above you in PvP.
Example: If you are ranked #15, you must challenge and defeat the player at #14 in an official FT7 match to take their spot and gain points!
