📜 OFFICIAL SERVER & LEADERBOARD RULES

1. Real-Time In-Game Tracking: All player stats, kills, deaths, and ELO ratings are registered live from our MCPE 0.15.10 PocketMine server. Whenever a kill is completed in-game, the server plugin verifies the match result and updates this website immediately.

2. Fair Play & Modded Clients: Hacks, reach modifications, auto-clickers, macro tools, or illegal client patches are strictly prohibited. Any player caught utilizing illicit modifications will face a permanent global ELO wipe and server ban.

3. Kill Farming & Exploits: Intentionally killing alternate accounts (alts), AFK players, or trading kills to boost ELO points or reach higher tier bands is banned. Suspicious match histories are audited by server staff.

4. Screen Recording Verification: Official competitive duels require full, unedited screen recordings to be submitted to our Discord server upon staff request.

📈 HOW RANKINGS & ELO WORK

1. Real-Time Server Sync: As soon as you kill a player inside the server, the server plugin calculates your new ELO points and pushes the update directly to the website database (`data.json`).

2. Tier Bands (C to S+): Players are grouped automatically into tier bands based on their current rating. Increasing your ELO moves you higher across the tier bands (from C up to S+).

3. Automatic ELO Sorting: Inside each tier band, players are ordered from highest to lowest ELO rating.

4. Auto-Refresh: The website polls for updates every 60 seconds and updates your standings automatically without needing to refresh your browser.
